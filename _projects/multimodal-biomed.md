Recent work looks at how multimodal models fail when one modality dominates, and at practical multimodal screening systems.

This includes *When Multimodal Models Stop Listening* (MMFM-BIOMED @ CVPR 2026) and *MultiSense-Pneumo* for pneumonia screening in resource-constrained settings.
