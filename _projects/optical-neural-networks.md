I contribute to research on compact and efficient optical neural network designs. The goal is to move parts of AI computation into the optical domain so imaging and inference can happen with lower latency and energy cost.

This work sits in the Wadduwage Lab and connects to differentiable microscopy and biomedical imaging systems.
