My bachelor's thesis and related papers focus on aspect-based sentiment analysis (ABSA) for reviews and other textual feedback.

The work covers category extraction, Instruct-DeBERTa, and comparative studies of ABSA methods, with publications at PACLIC, WiNLP/EMNLP, MERCon, and ICTer.
