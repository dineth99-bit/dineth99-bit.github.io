I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at [Old Dominion University](https://www.odu.edu/), working with [Dr. Dushan Wadduwage](https://wadduwagelab.com/) in the Wadduwage Lab. My research sits at the intersection of applied machine learning, biomedical imaging, causal inference, and natural language processing.

I graduated with First Class Honours in Electrical and Information Engineering from the [University of Ruhuna](https://eng.ruh.ac.lk/), Sri Lanka, with the highest OGPA in my batch (3.94/4.00). I also spent time as a software engineer at Codegen International, where I built LLM applications, RAG pipelines, and enterprise software.

**Research interests:** applied ML, AI for biomedical applications, out-of-distribution detection, differentiable microscopy, optical neural networks, and domain-specific NLP.

A more complete record is on my [CV](/cv/) and [publications](/publications/) pages. You can also find me on [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=605obQkAAAAJ&hl=en).
